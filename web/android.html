PocketPad for Android

Version 1.1 · The same PS5 and Xbox layouts, now with three themes, rich animations, connection haptics and an interactive getting-started guide.

Download Android app · APK

Android 8 or newer · Personal local install · No account

  1. Download the APK on your phone. If Android asks, allow your browser to install this app. It updates your existing PocketPad installation.
  2. Open PocketPad and choose Scan QR code.
  3. On your PC, open Connect phone and select Connect controller. Scan that QR code from the app.
  4. Tap Start controller. Open Settings for themes, animations and the replayable guide.

Your PC host stays open

The Android app connects to this PC over Wi-Fi, USB tethering, or Bluetooth PAN. Preview controllers and the practice guide work offline. Game-native touchpad input uses PlayStation/DS4 output.
